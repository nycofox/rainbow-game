# Rainbow Magic ✨

A tiny, colorful browser playground for young children. Move a giant rainbow star around a dark, starry sky and click to make confetti and glowing fireworks, complete with cheerful sound effects.

## Features

- Oversized, colorful mouse pointer
- Fireworks and confetti on **left or right click** (also works with touch)
- Playful sounds, with a mute button
- Fullscreen mode for distraction-free play
- Runs **offline**, with no dependencies or installation

## Play

1. Open `index.html` in a modern browser (Firefox or Chrome).
2. Click **Let's Play!** to start and enter fullscreen.
3. Move the mouse and click anywhere to make magic.

Use the **Sound** and **Fullscreen** buttons at the top of the screen. Press **Esc** to leave fullscreen.
